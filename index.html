🔄 Notable Moves

Dianna's DoubleDeez won the Keenan Allen sweepstakes this week — NeitherMoneyNorGoodPick cut him loose Thursday, and both Dianna's and Vladimir Tuten filed waiver claims for him Saturday afternoon; Dianna's claim won out (Devin Singletary was the corresponding drop). Keenan Allen — long known around this league as "the DWI guy" — delivered immediately: 6 catches, 63 yards, and a touchdown in the Colts' 19-17 win over Houston.

Real-world news worth flagging: J.J. McCarthy, stashed on NeitherMoneyNorGoodPick's bench, was traded from the Vikings to the Giants this week (for a 2027 5th-round pick) after New York lost starter Jaxson Dart to a season-ending knee injury. He opens as Jameis Winston's backup, but it's a real path to snaps for a young dynasty QB that wasn't walking into one in Minnesota.

Tougher real-world news for Fuck RIT: De'Von Achane, who managed only 1.7 points before leaving Sunday's game early, was confirmed afterward to have torn his ACL against the Chiefs and will miss the rest of the season — a real hit to the dynasty value of a roster currently sitting at #4 in the Power Rankings.

Otherwise a quiet week on the wire: Tucking DicK added Demarcus Robinson, Vladimir Tuten continued shuffling their bench (dropped Isiah Pacheco, added Matt Gay and Kendrick Bourne), Ashton Bottom Jeantys swapped out both their kicker (Harrison Mevis for Spencer Shrader) and a bench back (Mason Taylor for Emari Demercado), and Jaxson Eat My Dartbox added Isaiah Williams.
