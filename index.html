⚔️ This Week — Matchups

Fuck RIT def. Love on the Spectrum 193.98 – 167.8
Love actually had the bigger Thursday (85.8 to RIT's 53.1) behind a 40-burger from Josh Allen, and carried that lead through the early Sunday window too — up nearly 43 points at one point. Then the whole thing flipped in the late window alone: CeeDee Lamb led a 80-point Sunday-afternoon explosion for RIT while Love's late slate went cold (11.1), and a comfortable lead turned into a 26-point loss without either team playing a single snap after 4pm.
Tucking DicK def. Dianna's DoubleDeez 130.78 – 115.46
Dianna's actually put up the single biggest window of the game — 86.2 points in the early Sunday slate, the most either team scored anywhere. But Tucking DicK's own early output (81.8) kept it close, and Patrick Mahomes' Sunday night (28.98) was the difference-maker, pushing them ahead before Dianna's Monday piece (Cam Skattebo, 9.5) had a chance to answer. Fourth straight loss for the commissioner.
Vladimir Tuten def. Jaxson Eat My Dartbox 131.44 – 123.86
Built almost entirely in one window — Vlad's Sunday early slate alone (102.4, Rashod Bateman and Chris Olave both going off) nearly doubled Jaxson's early output (52.9). Jaxson actually won the rest of the day outright, outscoring Vlad 71.0 to 29.1 across the late and night windows, but the early hole was too deep to climb out of.
The DynaSteve def. In Puka & Egbuka We Trust 222.46 – 118.72
Biggest margin of the week, and it wasn't close by Sunday afternoon — DynaSteve's late window alone (94.8, Jaxon Smith-Njigba's 42.5 leading the way) outscored In Puka's entire day to that point. Then a Monday Night Rams stack — Kyren Williams, Davante Adams, and Matthew Stafford, all in the same game — combined for 82.2 more points to bury it. In Puka never had an answer for a roster hitting on every window.
NeitherMoneyNorGoodPick def. Ashton Bottom Jeantys 166.62 – 152.38
The week's family feud, part two. NeitherMoney built the whole margin early — Amon-Ra St. Brown's Thursday (35.2) plus a solid early Sunday (49.7) had them well ahead before Ashton Bottom even got going. Ashton mounted a real charge — Jonathan Taylor and Kenneth Walker combined for 65.3 points in the Sunday night window, the single biggest window either team put up all day — but the deficit built across the rest of Sunday held up by 14.24.

🔄 Notable Moves

Love on the Spectrum and Vladimir Tuten swapped tight ends — Dalton Kincaid to Love, Roman Wilson to Vlad — with Love also sending Vlad a 2027 2nd-round pick to sweeten it. A real trade this time, not one of Vlad's usual lopsided offers.

A quiet week otherwise on the waiver front: Dianna's DoubleDeez cycled through a few depth adds (Xavier Hutchinson, Devin Singletary), Vladimir Tuten swapped out Sean Tucker and Kendre Miller for Emari Demercado and DeMario Douglas, and In Puka & Egbuka We Trust added Darnell Mooney off waivers.
