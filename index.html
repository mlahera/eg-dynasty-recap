This Week — Matchups

Fuck RIT def. Dianna's DoubleDeez 154.26 – 134.22
Dianna's looked like the sharper build all day — over 100 of their 134 points were banked before the early Sunday window even closed, and for a while this had laugher written all over it. But RIT played it patient: modest Thursday production, a slow build across both Sunday windows, and enough left over after dark to run it down. Rough way for the commissioner to open a title defense — burn bright early, get caught by dinner.
Tucking DicK def. Love on the Spectrum 215.24 – 167.96
Never really in doubt. Tucking DicK had nearly 100 points in the bank before the 4pm window even started, then let Monday Night Football (Derrick Henry, 35.3) pad the final margin for sport. Love on the Spectrum had a legitimately good day themselves — Allen and Swift both popped in the early window — they just spent all of it chasing a team that was already gone. Maybe stop complaining about tanking and start drafting like it.
The DynaSteve def. Jaxson Eat My Dartbox 203.1 – 105.88
Dead on arrival. The reigning champ came out of Thursday night with 46 points already on the board and never let off, turning it into the week's most lopsided final margin. The Dartbox roster — old and about as active as a museum exhibit most weeks — had no version of this game where it wasn't a blowout by kickoff Sunday.
Ashton Bottom Jeantys def. Vladimir Tuten 171.22 – 148.98
The one game with a real pulse. Vlad built a legit lead through the early Sunday window — nearly 100 points banked, looking like the guy who finally wins with the roster instead of just the trade machine — but Ashton Bottom was sitting on a stacked Monday Night slate that hadn't kicked off yet. 44 points arrived after everyone else had already gone home for the week, and a would-be loss became a real, numbers-backed win. The spreadsheet said wait for Monday. The spreadsheet was right.
In Puka & Egbuka We Trust def. NeitherMoneyNorGoodPick 159.26 – 145.2
Tight all day, decided late. NeitherMoney built a real floor through Sunday and even got a night-window boost of their own — this one felt winnable into the evening. But In Puka's freshly upgraded backfield (Aaron Jones arrived via trade this week) answered with its own Sunday night surge to pull ahead in the final window. The self-proclaimed savant will need to find that same finishing gear.
