🚨 The League Starts Picking Sides

Six weeks in, people are starting to choose sides. The DynaSteve, Love on the Spectrum and Fuck RIT spent the week buying. Ryan (the artist formerly known as Vladimir Tuten, now Bowers Deep in Ass Jr.) and Wimmer spent it selling, and on Sunday neither of them bothered filling out a full lineup. The prize is Jeremiah Smith at 1.01.

Here's the catch. A bad record only gets you into the toilet bowl, and the 1.01 goes to whoever loses that. Joe already wrote the playbook last year: start a defense that's going to go negative, take the L, collect the pick. So you can tank for ten weeks and still get out-tanked in December. And every week you tank is a week you're not in the running for top-scorer money. Is it worth it? It's up to you 🤷🏻‍♂️

For the record, tanking is allowed under the league rules, so nobody's breaking anything. That hasn't stopped the group chat from arguing about whether it should stay that way.
